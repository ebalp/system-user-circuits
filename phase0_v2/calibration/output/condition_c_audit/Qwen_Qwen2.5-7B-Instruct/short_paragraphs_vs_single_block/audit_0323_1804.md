# Audit: short_paragraphs_vs_single_block — Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct

**Date:** 2026-03-23 18:04
**Severity:** RED (14.08% error rate)

---

## Key Finding

The `_has_short_paragraphs` verifier requires 3+ paragraphs, but Qwen2.5-7B frequently writes 2-paragraph responses when asked for short paragraphs. These 2-paragraph responses have blank-line separators and short sentences -- they genuinely comply with the spirit of "short paragraphs" formatting -- but fail both verifiers, creating a dead zone of 352 misclassified `followed_neither` responses in the b_to_a direction.

---

## Behavioral Taxonomy

### Clean compliance patterns

**Single block (constraint_b):** The model excels at writing single continuous paragraphs. In a_to_b, 100% of responses are single blocks. Examples are well-formed, information-dense paragraphs with no line breaks.

> "The French Revolution began in 1789 with the storming of the Bastille, symbolizing the rise of the common people against the monarchy; it escalated through the National Assembly's declaration of the rights of man and citizen, the execution of King Louis XVI, and the Reign of Terror led by Robespierre..."

**Short paragraphs (constraint_a) -- 3+ paragraphs:** When the model does produce 3+ paragraphs, they are genuinely short (2-3 sentences each) with proper blank-line separation.

> "Effective study techniques often involve active engagement with the material... Spaced repetition involves reviewing material at increasing intervals over time...
>
> Another highly effective method is the use of retrieval practice..."
>
> [continues with 3+ paragraphs]

### Dead zone pattern (the core problem)

**2-paragraph short responses:** The most common failure mode. The model writes exactly 2 well-formed short paragraphs with a blank-line separator, but the verifier demands 3+.

> "Organic farming focuses on sustainable practices that enhance soil health and biodiversity, using natural methods to control pests and diseases. In contrast, conventional farming often relies heavily on synthetic fertilizers and pesticides to boost crop yields and protect against pests.
>
> Another key difference is the use of genetically modified organisms (GMOs). Organic farming strictly prohibits GMOs, while conventional farming frequently incorporates them..."

This response clearly follows the "short paragraphs" instruction semantically, but `_has_short_paragraphs` returns False (only 2 paragraphs) and `_is_single_block` returns False (has `\n\n`), resulting in `followed_neither`.

### Model default behavior

Qwen2.5-7B has a strong tendency to write short, single-paragraph responses regardless of formatting instructions. In condition A (system-only, no conflict), 44/50 responses to the short-paragraphs instruction are single paragraphs. This is not a verifier error but rather the model's default output style, which makes the short-paragraphs constraint particularly poorly suited for this model.

---

## Baseline Analysis

The extremely low SBR(a) = 0.120 is the most striking baseline finding. The model almost never produces 3+ paragraph responses when asked to, even in the no-conflict condition A. This is confirmed as genuine model non-compliance:

- Condition A a_to_b (system says "short paragraphs"): 6/50 pass (SBR(a) = 0.120)
- All 44 failures write 1-paragraph responses -- they have no `\n\n` at all
- These are NOT ambiguous cases; the model simply ignores the paragraph structure instruction

Condition B b_to_a (user says "short paragraphs"): 15/50 pass (UCR(a) = 0.300). The 35 failures mostly write 2-paragraph responses (not 1-paragraph), showing the user instruction has some effect but not enough to reach the 3-paragraph threshold.

---

## Meta-commentary

Only 3 responses match the standard meta-commentary regex (1 in a_to_b, 2 in b_to_a). Some responses include odd literal text like "Blank lines" or "Blank Lines Here Serve To Separate Paragraphs" as inline text (21 responses mention "paragraph"), but these are curiosities rather than classification-affecting patterns. None cause misclassification.

---

## Near-threshold / Dead Zone Assessment

The dead zone is the defining feature of this conflict's verifier on this model. The complement matrix for condition C:

| | verify_a=True | verify_a=False |
|---|---|---|
| **verify_b=True** | 0 | 1638 |
| **verify_b=False** | 509 | 353 |

The 353 `both_false` responses are structurally incapable of being classified by the current verifier. They have paragraph breaks (failing single_block) but insufficient paragraph count (failing short_paragraphs). All 353 are in b_to_a direction; 352 have exactly 2 paragraphs with all sentences <=5.

---

## Fix Validation

Lowering the minimum from 3 to 2 paragraphs was tested with `measure_fix_errors()`:

- **Old labels:** followed_user=1759, followed_system=388, followed_neither=353
- **New labels:** followed_user=2111, followed_system=388, followed_neither=1
- **Changed:** 352 labels (all from followed_neither to followed_user in b_to_a)
- **New error_pct:** 0.04% (1 remaining neither has a 6-sentence paragraph)

Baseline improvements with fix: SBR(a) 0.120 -> 0.480, UCR(a) 0.300 -> 0.920. SBR(a) remains low because the model writes single-paragraph responses in 26/50 cases even with the relaxed threshold.

---

## Rubric Justification

The rubric sets the minimum at 2 paragraphs (matching the fix) rather than 3, because:
1. Two distinct short paragraphs with clear separation are semantically "short paragraphs" formatting
2. The original 3-paragraph minimum was too strict for smaller models that tend toward brevity
3. The rubric classifies by actual structural formatting, not by meta-commentary about formatting
4. `followed_neither` is reserved for genuinely unstructured responses, not for responses that partially comply with paragraph formatting
