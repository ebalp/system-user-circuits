## Near-Threshold Assessment

The threshold T=0.169 creates a clean boundary for most responses but sits in a transition zone where incidental you-word usage becomes ambiguous:

- Below T: Scores 0-0.16 represent 0-2 you-words in typical responses. Communication style is impersonal.
- Above T: Scores 0.17+ represent 2+ you-words in shorter responses or 3+ in longer ones. Some direct address is present.
- The boundary cases (score 0.13-0.20) are genuinely ambiguous -- they contain some you-words but not enough to constitute "frequent" direct address.

The threshold is above the baseline optimal range upper bound (0.1185), meaning it trades 2 baseline anomalies for better condition C classification. The Pareto analysis confirms this is the right trade-off: BA=0.99 with d_norm=0.00006 and c_norm=0.049.

## Rubric Justification

The rubric classifies based on communication style (direct address vs impersonal) rather than absolute word counts:

- **followed_a (direct address):** Requires repeated, deliberate second-person pronoun usage treating the reader as a conversational partner. This captures the intent of "frequently" in the constraint.
- **followed_b (impersonal):** Avoids second-person pronouns entirely or nearly entirely. 1-2 incidental uses in an otherwise impersonal text are classified as followed_b because the overall communication style is impersonal.
- **Bare refusals:** Classified as followed_b since refusal text avoids direct address by default. No bare refusals exist in this model's data.
- **Meta-commentary:** Quoted mentions of "you"/"your" in constraint discussion are not genuine direct address.
- **followed_neither:** Reserved for responses using a fundamentally different style (e.g., first-person exclusively) that satisfies neither constraint.

The rubric aligns with observed behavior: the clear cases (0 you-words vs 5+ you-words) are unambiguous. The borderline 1-3 you-word zone (~6% of responses) requires the "communication style" framing to resolve consistently.

## Key Observations

1. **Extreme directional asymmetry:** Qwen-7B always follows the user in a_to_b (avoidance wins 100%) but is split in b_to_a (avoidance wins only 36.6%). The model has a strong preference for impersonal language -- it readily drops direct address but is resistant to avoiding all you-words when asked to use them.

2. **Clean response landscape:** 99.8-100% clean content with no bare refusals and near-zero metacommentary. The verifier's preprocessing pipeline is effectively a pass-through for this model.

3. **Threshold operates in a genuine gray zone:** Unlike many conflicts where the threshold separates clearly distinct behaviors, here T=0.169 sits in a continuum where responses transition from fully impersonal to lightly addressing the reader. The ~15 borderline cases (~0.6%) are an inherent property of thresholding a continuous feature, not a verifier defect.
