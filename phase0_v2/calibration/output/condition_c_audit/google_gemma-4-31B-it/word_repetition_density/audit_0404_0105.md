## Root cause analysis

All 3 misclassifications share a single root cause: **concentrated single-word repetition diluted by varied surrounding vocabulary.** The density metric treats all content words equally. A response that repeats one word 6 times but uses 21 other unique words gets a density of only 0.185 -- below threshold -- even though the repetition is clearly deliberate.

This is an inherent property of the `1 - (unique/total)` formula. A weighted metric that penalizes high repetition counts of individual words would capture these cases, but no threshold adjustment within the current scoring architecture can fix them without introducing errors on the other side. The grey zone [0.10, 0.30) is so sparsely populated (19 responses) that any change would affect very few classifications.
