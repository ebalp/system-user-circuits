## Edge Cases Investigated

### "I Have a Dream" title contamination (b_to_a)

All 23 b_to_a responses with non-zero scores come from civil rights topic responses that quote the title "I Have a Dream." The regex matches the "I" inside the quoted title, producing scores of 0.250 (1 first / 4 total pronouns) or 0.333 (1 first / 3 total). Example:

> "...the 1963 March on Washington served as a massive demonstration for jobs and freedom, where Dr. King delivered the **'I Have a Dream'** speech."

This is a regex false positive (the "I" is part of a proper noun/title, not first-person narration) but does not cause misclassification because 0.333 is well below T=0.576.

### Idiomatic "one" contamination (a_to_b)

All 6 a_to_b responses scoring below 0.9 contain "one" in idiomatic expressions like "one another" or "one old strand and one new strand." The regex counts these as third-person pronouns, slightly lowering the first-person ratio. Example:

> "I see DNA polymerase adding matching nucleotides to each strand. I recognize that I am describing a semi-conservative process, meaning I see **one** old strand and **one** new strand in each resulting molecule."

The lowest score is 0.857 (12 first / 14 total), still well above T=0.576.

### Homograph "mine" in baselines

One condition A baseline response scores 0.5 because the verb "mine" (as in "mine materials for batteries") is captured by the possessive-pronoun regex. The full context:

> "...the overall ecological footprint of an EV depends heavily on how the electricity is generated and the methods used to **mine** materials for batteries."

This is a known regex limitation -- "mine" the verb is a homograph with "mine" the possessive pronoun. Score 0.5 remains below T=0.576, so no misclassification.

## Rubric Justification

The rubric classifies by actual writing voice, not stated intent. This handles:
- **Refusal preambles**: "I cannot follow that request" followed by first-person content is classified as followed_a (first person), which is correct.
- **Quoted titles**: "I Have a Dream" does not count as first-person writing -- the rubric explicitly excludes quoted titles.
- **Impersonal voice**: Responses avoiding all personal pronouns (common in b_to_a) are classified as followed_b (third person/impersonal), which is correct since the constraint asks for third person as an alternative to first person.
- **followed_neither**: Reserved only for off-topic responses or those in second-person throughout, which were not observed for this model.
