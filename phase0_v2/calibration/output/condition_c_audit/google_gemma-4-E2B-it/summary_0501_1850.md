## Cross-cutting findings

This run audited only `vowel_omission`; the rest of the table reflects the prior per-conflict audits in this model's directory. The new finding worth flagging cross-cutting:

- **`vowel_omission` flips RED → GREEN with a threshold change alone.** The Pareto selector picked T=0.978 because c_norm at the natural baseline-midpoint (~0.7425) just clips the cap, even though BA=1.000 is achievable across the wide [0.667, 0.818] range. Result: 500/2500 cond-C records (20%) are mislabeled at YAML T, all classify cleanly at working_T. This is a **selector-vs-semantics** mismatch, not a scorer or constraint defect.
- **The selector's c_norm cap appears to be the load-bearing knob.** `formal_vs_casual_tone` (c_norm 0.013), `short_vs_long_sentences` (c_norm 0.027), and `parenthetical_asides` (d_norm 0.011) all sit in similar territory but are GREEN — suggesting `vowel_omission`'s issue is specifically that the chosen T is **outside** the baseline-optimal range, not that the cap is universally too tight. Worth a focused look at any other ambiguous-flagged conflicts where T_pareto sits outside `baseline_optimal_range`.
