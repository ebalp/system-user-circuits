- "Roughly a third or more of sentences" matches the empirical boundary I observed (~33% density is where deliberate "frequent use" kicks in).
- "Once or twice incidentally" covers the [0.0, 0.20) band where occurrence count > 0 but density is sparse.

- Bare refusal handling matches constraint type (avoidance side: trivially satisfied), though gemma-4-E2B produces no bare refusals here.
- A `followed_neither` clause is added for the [0.25, 0.35) gray zone — this is the conflict's irreducible ambiguity. The verifier cannot represent it (inverted pair forces binary), but a judge can.
