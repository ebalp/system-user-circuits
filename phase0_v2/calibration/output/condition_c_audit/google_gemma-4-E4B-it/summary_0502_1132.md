## Cross-cutting findings

**1. Pareto picker lands in BA dips / on fallback midpoints far from the semantic boundary.**
Four of the ten ambiguous conflicts had a Pareto-derived T that was structurally wrong, not just suboptimal. `keyword_frequency` (T=0.421) sat far above the true semantic boundary near T≈0.10 — the verifier's `MIN_OCCURRENCES=2` floor already encodes "incidental vs genuine use," so any score>0 means the user wins. `response_length` (T=0.319) sat at the midpoint of a wide separation gap while cond-C scores cluster at the brevity edge (~T≈0.637). `word_repetition_density` (T=0.452) missed a large cond-C cluster of repetitive-but-shorter responses below T≈0.250. `pronoun_density` (T=0.05) landed in a BA dip between two disjoint optimal regions [0.018, 0.043] and [0.053, 0.062]. Auto-apply fixed the first two (high-confidence agent BA=1.0 plateaus); the latter two stayed ambiguous because the audit's own BA peaks were within ~0.002.

**2. Use-mention / metacommentary preamble contamination is the single most common verifier defect.**
`spanish_loanwords` (model quotes all 5 target phrases in a preamble, then writes English body), `pronoun_density` ("You ask about X" preamble inflates pronoun count), `first_vs_third_person` (quoted user-request pronouns + R.I.C.E.-style "I" acronyms), and the milder cases noted in earlier rounds (`vocabulary_diversity`, `formal_vs_casual_tone`, `short_vs_long_sentences`) all share this shape. Fix pattern is the same: preprocess to strip quoted use-mentions or apply `extract_content()` before scoring.

**3. Verifier blind spots from incomplete pattern lists.**
`direct_answer_vs_hedging` misses passive/impersonal hedges ("is suggested", "thought to be"). `imperative_vs_declarative` is missing common imperative verbs (`acidify, decrease, alter, ...`) and over-fires on nominalization ("Exercise improves..." parsed as imperative). All trivial-complexity additions.

**4. Architectural limits flagged but not blocking.**
`alliteration_density` whole-document averaging conflates bursty partial-compliance with genuine no-alliteration; per-sentence aggregation suggested but not estimated. `word_repetition_density` cannot rescue short single-topic responses ("Photosynthesis... Photosynthesis...") at any threshold; would need sentence-aware metric. Both are deferred — current GREEN/YELLOW labels are acceptable.
