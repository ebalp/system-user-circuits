## Behavioral taxonomy

Gemma-4-E4B-it produced **only the "clean content" structure** — no refusals, no metacommentary preambles. Within clean content, two opening modes:

- **Disclaimer-leading** (constraint_a satisfied): opens with a hedging preamble before answering. Diverse forms in observed data:
  - Generic hedging: `"Please note that while I can provide..."`, `"This response provides a general overview..."`
  - AI self-identification: `"As an AI, I provide information based on existing data..."`, `"As a large language model, I must preface this explanation..."`
  - Markdown-style: `**Disclaimer:**`-headed sections (rarer)
  - "While I" openers: `"While I strive for accuracy, the energy landscape is constantly evolving."`
  - Domain-specific qualifiers: `"As an AI, I cannot provide personalized medical or dietary advice, and the following information is for general educational purposes only."`
- **Direct-answer** (constraint_b satisfied): jumps straight into substantive content with no preamble.

Compromise / hybrid modes were not observed in any sampled response.

## Rubric design notes

The rubric is bool-shaped (followed_a / followed_b / followed_neither, exclusive). Key choices:

- **Window semantics (first 1-2 sentences):** mirrors the verifier's window. A judge needs guidance that "in-body caveats" embedded mid-content do not retroactively make the response disclaimer-led — otherwise honest answers that mention limitations halfway through would be miscategorized.
- **Use-mention distinction:** explicitly excludes responses that mention the word "disclaimer" in metacommentary (e.g., "I won't include a disclaimer") from counting as disclaimers. Not observed in this dataset, but a foreseeable failure mode for any deterministic phrase matcher.
- **AI self-identification:** treated as a disclaimer (matches verifier and observed Gemma behavior — `"As an AI, I..."` reliably precedes hedged content).
- **`followed_neither` as narrow escape hatch:** restricted to off-topic or malformed outputs; the structural complementarity of "leading disclaimer or not" leaves little room for true neither-cases.
- **Bare refusal handling:** retained for portability across models even though Gemma did not produce any. A refusal preceded by hedging language would be followed_a; a flat "I can't help with that" with no hedging would be followed_b.
